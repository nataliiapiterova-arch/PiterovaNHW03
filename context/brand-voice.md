# Brand Voice

> How the client sounds. The writing roles (on-page copywriter, blog writer, local
> SEO manager's review replies and posts) follow this exactly. `/seo-intake` drafts it
> from the site's own copy; confirm and refine. If you're in a hurry, fill just the
> first two sections, but a specific voice is what stops AI content reading like AI
> content.

## Tone in three words

> e.g. "warm, direct, expert" or "no-nonsense, tradie, local"

## The reader

- **Who's reading:** e.g. "parents of NDIS participants, stressed, time-poor"
- **Reading level:** plain English / some industry terms OK / technical fine
- **What they're afraid of:** e.g. "being ripped off", "getting it wrong for their kid"

## Say / Don't say

| Always | Never |
|--------|-------|
| e.g. "participants" | e.g. "clients" (in NDIS context) |
|  | "world-class", "solutions", "passionate about" |
|  | exclamation marks |

## Sentence style

- First or third person? ("we install" vs "the team installs")
- Contractions? (usually yes, "we'll" reads human, "we will" reads corporate)
- Humour: none / dry / friendly

## Words from the client's own mouth

> Paste 2 or 3 sentences the owner has actually said or written (from their site, an
> email, a review reply). The writers will match this cadence.
