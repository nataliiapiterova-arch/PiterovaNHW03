## Tone in three words

Warm, pampering, personal.

## The reader

- **Who's reading:** local women in the Lansing/East Lansing area booking self-care or a treat; also gift-buyers (partners, daughters) and small groups planning a spa day together.
- **Reading level:** plain English, light spa vocabulary is fine (facial, deep tissue, seaweed wrap), no medical or cosmetology jargon.
- **What they're afraid of:** paying spa prices for a rushed or impersonal experience; walking into a place that feels like a chain; awkwardness around tipping, what to wear, or trying a first-ever treatment (waxing, permanent makeup).

## Say / Don't say

| Always | Never |
|--------|-------|
| "relaxing", "rejuvenating", "pampering" (the site's own words) | "cheap", "discount" (undercuts the experience) |
| "customized facials" (their phrase) | "customised" (US spelling only) |
| Vada by name; the spa is personal and owner-led | "our team of experts" corporate-speak |
| "spa day", "treat yourself" | medical claims (anti-aging cures, skin condition treatment promises) |
| specific service names (hot stone massage, seaweed body treatment) | "world-class", "luxury redefined", "passionate about excellence" |

## Sentence style

- First person plural mostly ("we"), with Vada named where a personal touch helps ("Vada will tailor your facial to your skin").
- Contractions: yes. "You'll leave relaxed" reads human.
- Humour: none needed; warmth instead. Short, calm sentences. The copy should feel like the spa: unhurried but not padded.
