## Citation status: is Vada's the source AI answers pull from today?

**Partially — visible in directories, invisible in editorial sources, and fragmented as
an entity.** What the evidence shows:

**Where Vada's already surfaces (good news):**
- Vada's appears in Yelp's "Best 10 Day Spas in Lansing / East Lansing" result sets —
  the single most common source AI assistants cite for "best day spa in <city>" queries.
- `vadasdayspa.com/spa-day-packages` surfaces directly for spa-day-cost searches in the
  Lansing area, with concrete package prices ($160 / $210 / $240). This is exactly the
  kind of quotable pricing AI engines lift verbatim. It is the client's strongest
  GEO asset today.
- Mindtrip.ai (an AI travel-planning product) already carries a listing — proof that AI
  products are ingesting the entity — but under the name "Vada's Day Spa and Beauty
  Salon," not the canonical name.
- Review reputation is real: 4.6 stars / 128 reviews on Birdeye.

**Where competitors own the answer:**
- **Permanent makeup:** Aphrodite's Secret (East Lansing) dominates
  permanent-makeup/microblading searches with dedicated method pages (ombre, nano,
  microblading) and a strong Yelp presence. Vada's `/permanent-makeup` page appears but
  is outgunned.
- **Couples massage:** Zoë Life Spa and Salon has a dedicated couples-massage URL that
  surfaces for couples-massage queries; Vada's mentions couples massage only inside
  other pages.
- **The "10 best day spas in Lansing" editorial answer:** Revive Wellness (a
  competitor) publishes its own "Looking for day spas in Lansing, MI? 10 things you
  should know" article — a competitor literally owns the listicle format AI engines
  quote for this query. No independent editorial listicle (local news, Awesome Mitten,
  lansing.org blog) mentioning Vada's was found.

## Citability score: 44 / 100

The three biggest reasons for the score:

1. **Entity fragmentation (the single biggest drag).** At least five name variants and
   two duplicate profiles exist (details below). AI engines resolve businesses as
   entities; when the name, category, and even the city differ across sources, the
   engine's confidence drops and it cites a competitor with a clean entity instead.
2. **Zero third-party editorial coverage.** AI assistants answering "best day spa in
   Lansing" lean on listicles, local press, Reddit, and CVB pages. Vada's was found in
   none of these — only in self-serve directories. A competitor writes the listicle.
3. **Quotable facts exist but sit thin.** The package prices are excellent and already
   being surfaced; almost nothing else is quotable (no per-service prices found in
   search snippets, no durations for individual services, no named signature process,
   no owner-credential story surfaced where AI can find it).

Points were awarded for: real package pricing that already ranks, top-tier Yelp
directory presence, 14 years of operating history, and a genuinely strong review
profile (4.6/128 on Birdeye).

## Entity audit: the name-variant damage

Confirmed variants found in live listings this run:

| Name as listed | Where | Extra damage on that listing |
|---|---|---|
| **Vada's Day Spa** (canonical) | vadasdayspa.com, Yelp, Birdeye, Groupon, WellnessLiving (listing 1) | Yelp shows only 12 reviews vs 128 on Birdeye — review equity is stranded on Birdeye, and Yelp is what AI cites |
| Vadas Day Spa (no apostrophe) | facebook.com/VadasDaySpaEastLansing | This is one of **two** Facebook pages |
| Vada's Day Spa | facebook.com/vadasdayspasalon | Second Facebook page — duplicate social entity |
| Vada's Day Spa and Salon | ClassPass, chamberofcommerce.com, WellnessLiving (listing 2 — **duplicate profile**, separate URL `/vadasdayspaandsalon`) | Two WellnessLiving listings split the same business |
| Vada's Day Spa and Beauty Salon | Wheree, Atly, Mindtrip.ai | Wheree files them under **"Nail Salons"** — wrong category, dilutes "day spa" entity |
| Vada Day Spa | TripAdvisor, Instagram display name (@vadasdayspasalon) | TripAdvisor files them under **Lansing**, not East Lansing — wrong-city signal |

Additional entity hazards:
- An unrelated **"Vada Spa" in New York City** ranks on TripAdvisor for near-identical
  name queries. A fragmented local entity makes it easier for AI to blur the two.
- Address suite number appears as "#300" on some listings and is omitted on others
  (already flagged in client.md).
- The homepage title targets "Day Spa in Lansing, MI" while every authoritative listing
  says East Lansing — a self-inflicted location inconsistency.

**Verdict: this is the #1 GEO fix.** Pick the canonical name (recommend **"Vada's Day
Spa"** — shortest, matches domain and Yelp), then: merge/retire the duplicate Facebook
page, merge the duplicate WellnessLiving listing, correct TripAdvisor's city, correct
Wheree's category, and update ClassPass/Chamber/Atly/Mindtrip names. Hand this list to
the Local SEO Manager — it overlaps their citations plan.
