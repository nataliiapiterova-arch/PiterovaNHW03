## How much should you tip at a day spa?

Plan on tipping 15 to 20 percent of the regular service price at a day spa, given directly to your therapist, usually in cash.

That's the consensus from the [Spa Industry Association's tipping guide](https://dayspaassociation.com/spa-tipping-etiquette-how-much-should-you-tip/) and from [TODAY's spa etiquette guide](https://www.today.com/style/relax-all-spa-etiquette-questions-you-ve-ever-had-answered-t99886). On our packages, that works out to roughly $24 to $32 on the Mini, $32 to $42 on the Pampering, and $36 to $48 on the Half-Day. If more than one specialist takes care of you during a package, it's kind to split the tip between them.
