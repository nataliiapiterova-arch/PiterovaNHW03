## Why this order

Cost first because it converts an existing half-win into a locked-in citation (the GEO audit's #3 fan-out is the page most likely to earn an AI Overview citation first). Party second because it's the only fully uncontested query in the map. Comparison third because it repairs the biggest money-page gap (massage). The two guides follow because they widen coverage rather than open new fronts. Cost and comparison lead for a structural reason too: they're the formats competitors avoid publishing — nobody local prints prices, which is exactly why printing them wins.

## Publishing cadence

One post per week for five weeks is realistic for a single-owner spa, provided Vada's role is limited to answering each draft's short question set (10-15 minutes per post) rather than writing. If that's still too much, publish #1 and #2 this month and hold #3-#5 — the first two carry most of the citation value. Each post goes live only after the client answers its "Questions for the client" section and a second pass folds the answers in; nothing publishes at 60-70%.
