## Executive summary

- Vada's already owns the highest-value SERPs in its market: it ranks #1 organic for "day spa East Lansing MI" and top-3 for "spa packages Lansing" — the exact queries behind the client's primary goal (package bookings). Priority one is defending and converting that traffic, not chasing new terms.
- The biggest fixable gap is massage: Vada's has a /massages page but is invisible on "massage East Lansing" and "couples massage Lansing" SERPs, which are won by dedicated massage studios and by competitor Zoë Life's dedicated couples-massage page.
- Facials and lash/brow queries are being taken by med spas and specialists (Aurora Medi Spa, Aphrodite's Secret) with one-page-per-service local landing pages; Vada's competes with only its homepage. Dedicated service pages close this.
- Permanent makeup and waxing pages already rank page 1 — low-effort wins from strengthening what exists.
- Nail-salon queries (manicure/pedicure) are dominated by dedicated nail salons; the winnable angle is "spa pedicure/manicure" as part of packages, not head-on "nail salon East Lansing".

## Local modifiers

Ranked by evidence of use in winning titles and listings for this market:

1. **East Lansing, MI** — the true location; used by every specialist competitor that outranks Vada's on service queries. Primary modifier for service pages and GBP.
2. **Lansing, MI** — the bigger catchment; Vada's homepage already targets it and ranks. Keep on homepage/packages, don't strip it.
3. **near me** — dominant in the nail, wax, and massage verticals (Yelp/Fresha/StyleSeat aggregators all rank on it); won via GBP proximity + reviews, not on-page stuffing. Hand to Local SEO Manager.
4. **MSU / Michigan State / near campus** — competitors explicitly market to "the MSU and Greater Lansing Community"; student + visiting-parent demand (gift cards, graduation, parents' weekend).
5. **Okemos, Haslett** — adjacent suburbs; Skin Boss explicitly lists them in its service-area copy. Candidates for service-area mentions, not separate pages (single location).
6. **Greater Lansing / mid-Michigan** — regional phrasing used in competitor award/points-of-pride copy.
7. **48823 / Lake Lansing Rd** — NAP-level consistency terms for citations (note the open suite-number question in client.md).

Naming note for Local SEO Manager: directories show three name variants ("Vada's Day Spa", "…and Beauty Salon", "…and Salon"). Pick one canonical name; it affects every "near me" query above.

## Top 10 priorities

1. **Defend "day spa east lansing / lansing"** — #1 organic today; optimize homepage title/meta for CTR and booking, add LocalBusiness/DaySpa schema. Losing this loses the core funnel.
2. **Strengthen /spa-day-packages for "spa packages lansing mi"** — top-3 now, highest-ticket service, primary client goal; out-detail Zoë Life's /pkgs/ page (inclusions, duration, price anchors, booking CTA).
3. **Rebuild /massages to actually compete** — biggest visible gap; page exists but doesn't surface for "massage east lansing"; needs modality depth (Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone), therapist credibility, and internal links from homepage/packages.
4. **Create a dedicated facials page (geo-titled)** — the winning pattern (Aurora, Aqua Body Lab) is a standalone "Facials in East Lansing" page; the homepage alone leaves this on the table.
5. **Couples massage / couples spa page** — direct competitor gap vs Zoë Life with strong gift/occasion intent; confirm the service exists first.
6. **Strengthen /waxing** — already page 1; add full service menu structure to hold position against Bliss's geo page.
7. **Strengthen /permanent-makeup** — page 1 in a specialist SERP for a high-ticket service; add FAQ block (lasts how long, healing, vs microblading) to capture the question queries.
8. **Gift card page on-site** — demand is proven (third-party gift-card listings already rank for Vada's); capture it directly before the Q4 seasonal spike.
9. **FAQ/blog coverage of the spa-day question set** — "how much is a spa day", "what's included", "hot stone vs deep tissue"; feeds PAA and AI answers where no local competitor is present.
10. **Spa pedicure positioning inside packages** — don't fight nail salons head-on; target "spa pedicure east lansing" as a package component and upgrade narrative.
